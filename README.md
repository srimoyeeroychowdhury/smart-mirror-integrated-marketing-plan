# Smart Mirror Integrated Marketing Campaign
**Forage Virtual Experience – Omnichannel Marketing Project**

**Campaign Title:** Fitness That Meets You Where You Are

---

## Project Overview
This is a complete Integrated Marketing Creative Brief developed for the launch of a wall-mounted smart fitness mirror. The strategy focuses on introducing a category-defining product while championing the brand values of **community, inclusivity, and wellness**.

The brief was built through a structured research process using handwritten ICP notes, competitor analysis, Google Trends data (India), Instagram content audits, Meta Ads Library references, and YouTube search insights.

---

## What’s Included

- **Target Audience** & 3 Ideal Customer Personas (Priya Sharma, Alex Rivera, Jordan Lee)

## Key Strategic Highlights

- **Positioning**: Supportive, inclusive, and lifestyle-oriented fitness (differentiated from performance-heavy or clinical competitors)
- **Primary Channels**: Instagram (organic + paid) and YouTube
- **Core Message**: “Fitness that meets you where you are — in your living room, on your terms, and as part of a real, inclusive community.”
